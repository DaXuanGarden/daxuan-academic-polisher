# Optional style anchor

A user may provide one or two paragraphs inside a clearly labelled style anchor. Use it to
match register, sentence rhythm, information density, spelling variety, and authorial voice.
The anchor is a style sample, not a source of facts: never copy its claims, terminology,
examples, citations, or numbers into the manuscript.

Apply the anchor after the evidence-preservation pass. If it conflicts with a journal
instruction or would strengthen/weaken a claim, follow the instruction and evidence instead.
If no anchor is supplied, infer conservatively from the passage and requested venue.
