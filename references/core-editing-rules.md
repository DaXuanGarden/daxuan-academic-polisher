# Core editing rules

These rules apply to both Chinese and English output. Author instructions, target-journal
requirements, and factual accuracy outrank stylistic preferences.

## Preserve meaning and evidence

- Edit rather than rewrite. Preserve claims, claim strength, emphasis, point of view, tense,
  paragraph breaks, headings, lists, and voice unless the user asks for structural revision.
- Keep every fact, number, sign, unit, statistic, p-value, interval, sample size, identifier,
  figure/table reference, citation, limitation, and meaningful hedge. Do not reformat a
  citation merely to make it look uniform.
- Keep the source's terminology and spelling variety. One concept should keep one term unless
  the distinction is real. Do not rotate synonyms to avoid repetition.
- Default to the same length or shorter, but never shorten by removing content. Expand only
  when the user requests expansion or a necessary clarification cannot be expressed briefly.
- A local token check is an alarm for omissions or additions, not a proof of semantic
  equivalence. Read the revised passage for who did what, comparison groups, denominators,
  direction of effect, and the scope of every qualifier.

## Improve the argument's surface

- Lead with the paragraph's point or finding when the source supports that order. Put
  support, method, qualification, and caveat after the central claim.
- Prefer concrete subjects and verbs over nominalization, passive filler, and “the evaluation
  of ... was conducted” chains. Keep passive voice where the section or author convention
  calls for it.
- Remove throat-clearing, duplicated claims, empty intensifiers, generic significance
  statements, stacked hedges, and paragraph-final restatements.
- Calibrate reporting verbs to evidence. Do not turn “suggests” into “demonstrates”, or a
  measured association into causation. Do not weaken a result merely to sound cautious.
- Keep lists, contrast, and transitions only when they clarify an actual relationship. Do
  not force a three-item list, repeated “first/second/finally” frame, or identical openings.
- Treat anti-pattern word lists as review prompts, not a license to delete fixed technical
  terms. “Robust”, “significant”, “系统”, or “协同效应” stays when it has field-specific
  meaning and support.

## Minimal-edit ledger

Before finalizing, mentally account for each protected item and each claim: (1) source value
and unit, (2) subject and action, (3) comparison or direction, (4) uncertainty/limitation,
(5) citation or locator. If a rewrite cannot preserve one item, keep the original wording and
flag it instead of guessing.

## Scope boundaries

This skill edits language. It may flag a terminology, logic, or evidence problem visible in
the supplied text, but it does not verify the literature, recalculate statistics, judge
scientific validity, or decide whether a paper should be accepted. Those are separate tasks.
